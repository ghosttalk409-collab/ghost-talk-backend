Zero-Trace Memory State

Messages, files and call setup are end-to-end encrypted on your device before they leave it. Our server only relays unreadable ciphertext in memory: it never stores message content, and nothing is written to disk once a chat ends. To make accounts and subscriptions work we keep a minimal record: your anonymous Ghost ID, a one-way hash of your device secret, your plan status and expiry, and your optional custom handle.

Sovereign Peer Routing

Voice and video calls flow directly between peers over encrypted WebRTC connections, and the server only helps them find each other. Text and files are encrypted end to end and relayed as ciphertext, so even we cannot read them.

Cryptographic Isolation

Engineered with Old Money discretion. Every chat uses fresh keys that exist only in memory (ECDH P-256 with AES-256-GCM). Compare the security code with your contact to confirm nobody is intercepting. When you end a session the chat and its keys are erased from your device. Optional alerts wake your phone with a generic, content-free ping; messages and calls are never sent through the push service, and you can block anyone from reaching you.

Privacy Policy

Message content, call content and files are end-to-end encrypted and are never stored on our servers. We keep only an anonymous account record (Ghost ID, hashed device secret, plan status and expiry, optional handle, sign-up time and, if you arrived through a partner link, its campaign code). Payments are processed by NOWPayments; we receive only a payment confirmation. Like any online service, our hosting provider can see network-level information such as IP addresses of connections, and calls between two people connect directly unless a relay is used. Optional alerts (Android app): if you turn them on we keep your phone's push token until you turn them off. An alert only says "New secure message" or "Incoming secure call" and never contains a name, text or key; Google's push service can see that an alert reached your phone, not who sent it or what it was. So that a sender's app can deliver once you open the app, our server remembers in memory only, for 2 minutes, which Ghost IDs tried to reach you; nothing is queued. If you block someone we store only a one-way hash of their ID so they can no longer message, call or alert you.

No cookies, no tracking pixels, and no third-party telemetry are integrated into the Ghost Talk ecosystem.
